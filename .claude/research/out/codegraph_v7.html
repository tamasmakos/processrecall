OpenTelemetry settings

Three required, three forbidden. The forbidden three are not gates to switch off — the invariant says the ingester strips the fields regardless, so setting them changes nothing except the risk on the wire.

OTEL_LOG_TOOL_DETAILS=1● ok requiredwithout it TOUCHED is absent and template degrades to the tool name (AC 3)
